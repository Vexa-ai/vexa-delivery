title: "Vexa Enterprise"
---

Vexa Enterprise is a private release channel that keeps your air-gapped deployment always up
to date and in compliance with the product specification agreed for your company, guaranteed
by Vexa.

## Three moves

1. **Set up a generic environment** — a Kubernetes cluster, an OpenShift namespace, a managed
   cloud. Nothing Vexa-specific.
2. **Set up the channel** — one pull-only credential, one subscription.
3. **The software stays up to date** — inside your perimeter, on your schedule, within your
   specification.

Your cluster pulls; nothing reaches in. Every image is digest-pinned and signed, every release
carries evidence you can verify offline, and no release reaches your channel until it has been
rendered with your values and matched against your contract. Your production moves when you
move the pin.

Read [how it works](how-it-works), then start at [Install](install). Each
[environment](environments/kubernetes) page states what it must provide.
