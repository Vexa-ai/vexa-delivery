[Vexa](https://github.com/Vexa-ai/vexa) is open source and self-hosted — images on Docker
Hub, charts and code in the open repositories. You can run it without us, forever. Vexa
Delivery is the open-source machinery that keeps that deployment always up to date: a
signed channel your cluster pulls from — **the open channel, free for everyone**, or a
tailored channel operated with us. Nothing reaches in: every image is digest-pinned and
signed, verified inside your perimeter against keys you pin, and your production moves only
when you move the pin.

Two ways to stay current — same machinery, same kit, same verification:

## The open channel — free

Delivers the public releases exactly as published — Apache-2.0 images pulled from Docker
Hub, digest-pinned and signed. Subscribe your cluster and it stays current with upstream —
staging rolls automatically, you promote to production under your own attestation.
Community support.

## A tailored channel — operated with us

A private channel for your company. Releases arrive attestation-complete, with the full
evidence set — gate reports and station verdicts from real environments — and no release
reaches your channel until it has been rendered with your values and matched against the
contract agreed for your company. A support lane with response commitments sits behind it.

The channel is two-way, and the return path differs. On the open channel, the way back is
GitHub issues — plus any station report you choose to share. On a tailored channel, the
return path is contractual: a [ticket lane](support) with response commitments, and the
[telemetry rung you set](telemetry-ladder) — from silent to receipts to health counters to
diagnostics, never content. The more your side chooses to send back, the faster your
deployment improves — that bandwidth is what a tailored channel is for.

The software is identical on both paths — features never move behind a paywall — and
switching paths is a subscription change, not a migration.

## Three moves

1. **Set up a generic environment** — a Kubernetes cluster, an OpenShift namespace, a managed
   cloud. Nothing Vexa-specific.
2. **Set up the channel** — one pull-only credential, one subscription.
3. **The software stays up to date** — inside your perimeter, on your schedule.
