Co-design is dedicated Vexa engineering hours, allocated to you and spent before anything
breaks. [Support](support) fixes what is broken; co-design decides what to build.

## What the seat includes

| | |
|---|---|
| **Biweekly sessions** | A standing meeting with your team. Your pains reach us while they are small; patterns proven at organizations your size and larger reach you as they land |
| **Workflow design** | We map where meeting intelligence enters your processes, what your teams see, and what stays out |
| **Your engineers in Vexa OSS** | The core you run is Apache-2.0. Your team's contributions get first-class maintainer attention and return through the same validated channel |
| **Twice-yearly scoped sprints** | Agreed scope, our bandwidth, your priorities. General needs land in the product; needs specific to you live in your values, so they survive every upgrade |

The channel keeps your deployment current; co-design keeps it pointed at what your
organization needs from its meetings. Cadence and scope are set in your subscription
agreement.
