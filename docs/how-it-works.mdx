## The loop

1. **Publish** — images freeze and are signed.
2. **Gate** — rendered with your values, against your contract. See [the station gate](station-gate).
3. **Channel** — the gated entry lands; `current` moves.
4. **Pull** — your Argo CD, over your pull-only credential or your mirror.
5. **Admission** — Kyverno checks the signature and refuses anything not digest-pinned. See [verify](verify).
6. **Smoke** — a human watches the bot capture a real meeting. Dated receipt.
7. **Station report** — verdicts, redacted values, profile, contract; sent back.
8. **Back to 2** — that report is the station the next release is gated against.

<Note>
**Rung.** Step 6 is the intended procedure and the smoke tool implements it, but no audio has reached the pipeline in any delivery run yet — [what's proven, and where](tested#the-delivered-software).
</Note>

## What a release is

One OCI artifact:

- **`entry.json`** — version, source commit, image digests, and the sha256 of every evidence file.
- **`evidence/`** — digest map, delivery receipt from our own production run, SLSA provenance, Sigstore trust material for offline verification.
- **`VERIFY.md`** — the commands, with this release's filenames filled in.
- Beside it, the Helm chart as an OCI artifact, every digest baked into its values — including the images the runtime spawns per meeting.

Two fields are contractual: **`evidence_absent`** names what this release lacks, and **`break_glass`** names who authorised an incomplete chain, and why.

Each stage that validates the artifact signs it. The bytes never change; a candidate that fails stops accumulating evidence.

## Channels — open or private

Everything Vexa runs on is open source and published — images on Docker Hub, code and charts in the open.

- **The open channel** carries those public releases exactly as published: digest-pinned, signed, pulled from Docker Hub. The way back is GitHub issues.
- **Your private channel** is operated with us: releases arrive attestation-complete with the full evidence set, gated against your specification before anything reaches you. The way back is a ticket lane and the [telemetry rung you set](telemetry-ladder) — silent, receipts, health counters, never content.

The software is identical on both, and switching is a subscription change, not a migration.
